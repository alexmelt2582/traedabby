## Data and authentication facts

- Login state is read from
  `%APPDATA%\TRAE SOLO CN\User\globalStorage\storage.json`.
- The authoritative login key is `iCubeAuthInfo://icube.cloudide`.
- A complete snapshot also needs the matching `iCubeAuthInfo://icube-dc:*`
  device key, `iCubeAuthInfo://usertag`, and `iCubeServerData://*`.
- Entitlement data may be standalone or embedded in server storage. Keep both
  compatibility paths.
- Do not write `state.vscdb` for account switching in the current TRAE version.
- Account exports must remain encrypted; never add a plaintext fallback.
- Rollback state stays in memory. Do not write plaintext `storage.before.json`
  snapshots or legacy `state.before.vscdb` files.
- Account index check-in and keep-alive metadata are public metadata only. They must
  never be copied into authentication snapshots.

## Check-in invariants

- Endpoints:
  - `GET /trae/api/v2/ug/checkin_credits/status`
  - `POST /trae/api/v2/ug/checkin_credits/claim`
- The status and claim host is fixed to `https://api.trae.cn`.
- Do not substitute the account-specific `loginHost`; `api.trae.com.cn` caused a
  false HTTP 404.
- `x-device-id` and the status `did` query parameter must use the account's own
  stable `userId`. A shared machine-generated id causes “当前设备已签到”.
- The total daily reward is `credits`. `extra_credits` is only an extra component.
  Do not display 50 when the total is 150.
- The scheduler sweeps every 30 minutes and must skip already-checked accounts.
- Automatic check-in must skip while Cockpit Tools is running.

## Keep-alive invariants

- The default sweep is every 30 minutes; each account refreshes at most every six
  hours. Failures retry after 30 minutes.
- Inactive accounts may rotate their stored refresh token and then refresh usage.
- The active account must be synchronized from live `storage.json`; do not rotate
  its refresh token from the backup while TRAE is running.
- Keep-alive must be mutually exclusive with account switching, login, check-in,
  and insight refresh.
- Skip the whole keep-alive sweep while Cockpit Tools is running.
- The UI status meanings are:
  - `运行中`: active account maintained by the live TRAE session.
  - `正常`: latest keep-alive succeeded.
  - `待保活`: no keep-alive result yet.
  - `失败`: latest keep-alive failed and will retry.
